About the Center

Future Star Computer & Tuition Center (FSCC) is an evening learning center in Shahdadpur. We teach Programming, Office Automation, Graphics, English, Science and Mathematics in small groups and one-to-one special courses. Classes run Monday to Saturday from 4:00 PM.

Sir Sajjad Ali

Co-owner · Programming

Teaches Programming to boys (Monday–Wednesday) and girls (Thursday–Saturday), and one-to-one special Programming courses.

Sir Ahmed Raza

Co-owner · Office Automation & Graphics

Teaches Office Automation and Graphics, and one-to-one special courses in Graphics and Office.
